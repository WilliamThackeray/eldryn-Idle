# eldryn-Idle

## World Concept: A Shattered, Ever-Changing Realm

Long ago, the world was ruled by a vast and powerful magical empire—a civilization that bent the very fabric of reality to its will. Towers of impossible design, cities floating upon the aether, and entire landscapes shaped by arcane forces defined this once-glorious era. But something—whether hubris, a catastrophic spell, or an external force—caused the empire’s collapse. Magic, once controlled and refined, spiraled into chaos, warping the world itself.

Now, the land is in constant flux. Biomes shift unpredictably, mountains rise and fall, forests wither overnight only to bloom elsewhere, and entire dungeons appear and vanish as if the world itself is rewriting its own history. Those who survive in this unstable world must adapt, exploring regions that may never exist in the same form again.

### Core Mechanics & Progression

Shifting Landscapes – Locations move, vanish, and reappear, making exploration dynamic.
Resource Flux – Some materials only exist for limited time periods before fading away.
Dungeon Cycles – Ruins and vaults open and collapse, forcing adventurers to seek them out before they’re lost again.
Factions & Power Struggles – The remnants of the empire’s once-great rulers, scholars, and warlords battle to reclaim their lost dominance.
Arcane Artifacts & Lost Knowledge – Players uncover and piece together ancient relics, forgotten spells, and long-buried secrets to shape the new world.

### Thematic Inspirations & Naming Ideas

A world constantly reshaping itself is key to this concept.
The empire’s fall and recovery provides a narrative backbone for progression.
Magic is both the source of destruction and the key to mastering the world.
